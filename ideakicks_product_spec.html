1. Detailed Information Architecture (IA)

Structural taxonomy, global navigation hierarchy, and entity relationships

System IA
1.1 IA Architectural Principles
  • Persona-Centric Partitioning: Clean division between Creator (supply), Backer (demand), and Partner (ecosystem) paths.
  • Flattened Navigation Depth: Critical account actions (Sign out, Settings) accessible in ≤1 click from any route via global header.
  • Predictable Entity Hierarchy: Category → Campaign → Reward Tier → Backer Pledge → Fulfillment Updates.
  • Transparent Legal & Financials: Unified zero-surprises pricing (5% platform fee) accessible across all entry funnels.

3. End-to-End User Flows

Step-by-step logic maps for core platform personas

User Journeys

Flow 1: The Creator Journey (Ideation to Payout)

1

Discovery & Value Proposition

Creator lands on /start, reviews flexible funding model (keep what you raise) and 5% platform fee.

2

Wizard Application (/start/application)

Completes 4-step wizard: ID verification, media upload, funding target, and tier rewards definition.

3

Human Review & Coaching (<48h)

Editorial team reviews feasibility; 1-on-1 coach assigned to polish story, marketing, and reward pricing.

4

Public Launch & Campaign Operations

Project goes live on /explore. Creator manages backers, comments, and updates inside /creator-studio/:id.

5

Campaign Close & Stripe Escrow Release

Funds transferred via Stripe Connect. Creator sends regular manufacturing updates to backers until rewards delivered.

Flow 2: The Backer Journey (Discovery to Fulfillment)

1

Discovery & Filtering

Backer navigates /explore; searches by keyword, category, funding status, or "Ending Soon" urgency.

2

Evaluation on Campaign Page (/campaign/:id)

Reviews video pitch, creator credentials, prototype demos, estimated delivery dates, and transparent risk statements.

3

Pledge & Flexible Checkout (/campaign/:id/back)

Selects preferred reward tier; enters shipping destination and payment credentials. Order summary includes taxes & shipping.

4

Confirmation & Dashboard Tracking

Receipt sent to email; project automatically populated into user's /dashboard under "Backed Projects" tab.

5

Reward Fulfillment & Creator Updates

Receives notifications for manufacturing milestones, production photos, tracking numbers, and delivery confirmation.

Flow 3: Partner & Vendor Ecosystem Flow
  1. Ecosystem Landing: Service provider views /vendors to explore partnership benefits (manufacturing, fulfillment, legal, hardware prototyping).
  2. Inquiry Submission: Submits capability profile, factory certifications, minimum order quantities (MOQs), and client references.
  3. Verification Audit: Ideakicks team vets business standing and quality guarantees.
  4. Directory Listing: Verified partner added to internal roster; matched directly with creators needing execution help.
Flow 4: Account & Security Management (Header UX)
  1. Global Accessibility: User profile icon in header displays instant modal menu on click.
  2. One-Click Sign Out: Direct sign-out button eliminates nested navigation depth.
  3. Direct Settings Routing: Clicking "Settings" links to /dashboard#settings, auto-activating the settings tab.
  4. Profile & Security: Update bio, change notification frequencies, or export backer data with zero friction.
